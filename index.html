Established Legacy

"Building Community Before Building Product"

For over 15 years, Mangosteen Cafe has stood as a peaceful haven nestled near Mayank Blue Water Park Road in Indore. What started as a passionate dream to blend sustainable living with world-class coffee has grown into Indore’s beloved cultural and culinary landmark.

Whether you are looking for a tranquil nook to complete your remote work with our high-speed Wi-Fi, gathering with loved ones over our handcrafted 12-inch Ottoman Empire Pizza, or savoring an authentic Thai Green Curry bowl—Mangosteen welcomes you with open arms and rustic warmth.
